[Peers Touch](https://github.com/peers-labs/peers-touch) is a project I founded and have led from zero. Today's social networks lock people's relationships and data inside centralized servers — privacy is ceded to the platform, and the account is never truly yours. Peers Touch explores an alternative: a peer-to-peer, self-hostable federated network where people own their social graph and data. AI agents are the new capability of this era, yet the agents on the market are essentially product extensions of the large platforms; in the same spirit, we are building agent capabilities of our own — one that runs on the user's own node rather than as a service rented from a platform. The work moves along two lines: first, to build the social network itself on a decentralized foundation; and then, in the age of agents, to give each person's own social DNA native, extensible agent capabilities.
